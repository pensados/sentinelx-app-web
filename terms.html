What SentinelX is

SentinelX is a hosted service that lets you operate Linux servers via an LLM (Claude.ai, ChatGPT, etc.). You install a small open-source agent on your server, the agent connects to our hub, and the hub appears as an MCP connector in your LLM.

Your responsibility
